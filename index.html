About Dr. Okoro

A dedicated professional with a passion for helping individuals find peace, healing, and personal growth.

25 Years of Excellence

Dr. Blessing Okoro is a licensed psychologist with 25 years of experience, offering compassionate and personalized therapy. She employs a unique, integrative approach, blending Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Psychotherapy, Person-Centered, and other evidence-based modalities to deliver exceptional care.

Dr. Okoro's mastery of this eclectic approach empowers individuals navigating PTSD, trauma, behavioral challenges, stress, anxiety, life transitions, and other mental health concerns.

PhD Clinical Psychology UC Riverside Veterans Center Residency

Pioneering Virtual Care

Driven by compassion, she pioneered virtual therapy sessions during the COVID-19 pandemic, providing essential support during a critical time. Her nurturing approach helped countless individuals cope and find solace.
